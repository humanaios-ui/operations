🎯 WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

A professional research task marketplace where:
✓ Employers post tasks (writing, analysis, design, validation)
✓ Workers earn money directly (no extraction, transparent fees)

💰 THE MODEL

Per task:
  Employer pays: $500
  Worker receives: $465 (93%)
  Empirica commission: $25 (5%)
  Empirica net after Stripe: $14/task
  Verification bonus (returned to workers): $10

Phase 1 pilot (20-50 matches):
  Income: $280-700
  Expenses: ~$300
  Net: -$20 to +$400 (break-even or positive)

Phase 2 (if approved: 200 matches/month):
  Net: +$2.3K/month sustainable
