## Risk Mitigation

**What if grants are slow?**
- Start partnership pilots NOW (non-paying) to generate research traction faster
- Open customer conversations in parallel (months 3-4) to de-risk B2B timeline
- Fallback: Raise small Seed round ($250-500K) by month 6 to fund engineering

**What if research partnerships decline?**
- Not likely (ACAT is published + open), but plan for: pivot to direct university outreach
- Target CS + AI safety labs first (highest immediate need)
- Offer revenue-share models to incentivize dataset contribution

**What if board pressure to accelerate B2B?**
- Clarify: Building B2B parallel is OK, but don't starve academic pipeline
- Academic revenue = low-cost customer validation + cash flow for engineering
- Trade-off: Slower B2B launch, but with higher probability of customer fit

**What if recovery community needs urgent attention?**
- Clarify: Curriculum docs exist; infrastructure build can wait
- Proposition: Find dedicated founder for recovery pillar (separate entity, future funding)
- Keep HumanAIOS LLC as AI + research + B2B body; spin recovery into separate org if needed
