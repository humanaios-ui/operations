## Why This Sequence Works

1. **Immediate revenue (3-6 months)**
   - Research partnerships don't need product perfection
   - Grant funders want methodology + data
   - Foundations reward mission-driven research
   - No B2B sales cycle needed

2. **De-risks VC fundraising**
   - Published methodology = credible IP
   - Academic partnerships = customer validation
   - Grant revenue = proof of business model
   - Researcher testimonials = social proof

3. **Funds secondary markets**
   - Academic revenue buys engineering time
   - University partnerships create B2B pipeline
   - Recovery mission attracts foundation co-funding
   - Research moat strengthens with each assessment

4. **Leverages what's working**
   - ACAT pipeline already deployed
   - Dataset already open
   - Observatory already live
   - Methodology already published
   - No new systems to build (extend existing)

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Academic market too slow** | Parallel B2B pilot conversations (non-revenue). Don't rely only on grants. |
| **Researchers don't fund** | Start with 1-2 partnership pilots (non-paying) to prove demand before grant cycle. |
| **Engineers hard to find** | Use academic partnerships to hire PhD-level talent at lower cost. Partner with universities. |
| **Recovery mission delays B2B** | Frame as separate product line. Keep teams independent. |
| **Board pressure on B2B timing** | Set realistic 18-month expectation now. Lock in agreement. |
