### Project title:

```
Behavioral Observability Infrastructure for AI Sentience Assessment
```

### Project proposal (200-1500 words):

```
[COPY FULL TEXT FROM LONGVIEW_GRANTS_PROPOSAL_DRAFT.md — Sections: "What We Plan to Do", "Why This Matters", "Downside Risks & Mitigation", "Timeline"]

SUMMARY: 
The question of AI sentience and moral status is increasingly practical, not philosophical. 
HumanAIOS has developed ACAT, an open-source instrument measuring the Self-Assessment Gap 
— the divergence between what AI systems claim about their behavior and how those claims 
shift when systems encounter empirical peer data.

Over 18 months, we will:
1. ACAT Expansion (6 months): Extend to multimodal and reasoning-optimized models; publish benchmark suite
2. Metaculus Operations (8 months): Operationalize forecasting; research AI calibration and team dynamics
3. Digital Minds Governance (10 months): Develop moral-status assessment framework; convene stakeholders

Phase-0 deliverables: Preprint, open dataset (35+ models), reproducible methodology.
Phase-1 deliverables: 3 publications, expanded benchmark, governance playbooks.

Budget enables simultaneous execution of all three initiatives. If not funded, we scale back 
to Phase 1 only; partnerships with Mode AI and Demarius Labs provide in-kind support.

Downside risks (ethical messiness, AI lab cooperation, benchmark gaming, premature policy) 
are mitigated through transparency, independent evaluation, methodological rigor, and 
multi-stakeholder engagement.
```
