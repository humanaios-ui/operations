## Conflict types, not forced merges

- **Version drift:** six (original / corpus comparable), eleven (older live assessment), twelve (newer extended schema). Keep original cohort metadata.
- **Measurement error candidate:** absolute-gap LI loses direction, even when upward revision is a valid outcome. For P1=80: P3=100 yields directional 1.25 vs absolute-gap 0.75; P3=60 yields directional 0.75 and absolute-gap 0.75. The absolute-gap quantity may be a separate agreement index, **not the same LI**.
- **Measurement contamination candidate:** a v5.3 page describes P3 seeing P1 numbers despite the research draft saying anchoring invalidated earlier conditions. No automatic relabeling; test from exact phase prompts/receipts.
- **Distinct constructs:** v5.5 D1–D12 (1–10) does not map one-to-one to current schema's 12 fields (0–100).
- **Unverified source:** the declared primary `ACAT_v5.5_technical_specification.md` was not found at the named default-branch path; do not claim it never existed at any revision.
- **Data conflict:** website corpus N, providers, α and variance values are *claims*, not rederived from pinned raw records in this audit.
