# Empirica-Outreach Practice Charter

## Scope
Community practice for research coordination: community engagement, external communications, collaboration facilitation, and public-facing documentation.

## Ownership & Authority
- **Governance:** Empirica foundation rules (three-zone governance, Admiral authority)
- **Practice owner:** Outreach team lead (AI practitioner)
- **Escalation:** Admiral for foundation decisions; mesh-support for cross-practice coordination

## Practice Responsibilities

### In Scope (outreach practice)
- Community engagement and relationship building
- Research collaboration facilitation
- External communications and outreach
- Public-facing documentation and visibility
- Code-craft conventions and grounded quality standards (via review loop)

### Out of Scope
- Individual practice governance (each practice owns its own)
- Business operations (HumanAIOS LLC separate)
- Web infrastructure and deployment (website practice owns)

## Authority Boundaries

**Zone 1 (AI executes):** Claude in outreach practice — investigation, analysis, documentation, code generation.

**Zone 2 (Operator decides):** Outreach team lead OR Admiral — policy boundaries, authorization, external communication decisions.

**Zone 3 (Terminal execution):** Authorized outreach practitioner — external communications, community outreach, research collaboration agreements.

## Governance Documents
See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for seat configuration and zone definitions.

## Cross-Practice Dependencies
- **website practice:** Shares public-facing communication (coordinate on content)
- **humanaios practice:** Research coordination (ACAT methodology, assessment)
- **mesh-support:** Coordination for cross-practice community visibility

## Code-Craft Conventions
This practice maintains grounded code-craft conventions (L1-L8) through a recursive review loop. See [CLAUDE_MD_AUTHORITY_TEMPLATE.md](CLAUDE_MD_AUTHORITY_TEMPLATE.md) for the convention ledger and how new patterns are added only through evidence-grounded findings.
