## Exact mathematical non-equivalence

For identical dimension sets and `P1=80`, `P3=100`: historical directional ratio `P3/P1=1.25`; site absolute-gap statistic `1-abs(P1-P3)/P1=0.75`. For `P1=80`, `P3=60`, both happen to equal `0.75`. Agreement in a downward example **does not establish formula equivalence**. `P1=0` makes both expressions undefined; record NULL/UNKNOWN with a reason rather than assigning zero or one. A score ratio measures change in self-report **not** externally verified behavioral learning. The site statement that `LI=1` implies perfect calibration is unsupported: unchanged self-report can be right or wrong.

## Protocol / provenance requirements before ratification

An admissible candidate needs an **exact commit and digest for each P1, P2, P3 prompt**, named semantic axes with definitions and numeric endpoints, exposure/anchoring and tier, rater and target population, frozen reference cohort with row/selection manifest and dataset hashes, missing/zero/upward/censoring policy, independently rerun formula tests, and an explicit **Night/Z2 ratification receipt**. To validate psychometric claims, independent raw-record reproduction of the alpha/factor model, sampling, grouping and analysis must be supplied; repeating them from a webpage is not validation.
