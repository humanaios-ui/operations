## Attribution (separate from scientific endorsement)

- **Primary text:** *The Kybalion: A Study of the Hermetic Philosophy of Ancient Egypt and Greece*, published 1908 under the pseudonym "Three Initiates", explicitly presents the **Principle of Mentalism** as a philosophical/metaphysical claim.
  Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14209
- **Historical qualification:** The historical relationship between *The Kybalion* and earlier Hermetic traditions is disputed. It is not established as universal Freemasonry doctrine. Comparative research: Nicholas Chapel, *The Kybalion's New Clothes: An Early 20th Century Text's Dubious Association with Hermeticism*, published at https://www.hermeticulture.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/The-Kybalions-New-Clothes.pdf .
- **Evidence boundary:** Neither historical source establishes that thoughts alone cause physical outcomes, make grants available, qualify a person for a credential, or produce income. A meaningful analogy is *purposeful thought may motivate planning and action*. That is a proposed behavioral/process hypothesis, not an assertion of metaphysical causation.

## Human-approved implementation interpretation

User-directed practical lens:

```
INTENT (expressed human purpose)
  → HYPOTHESIS (possible path; source/relevance checked)
  → ACTION (human permission; independently recorded)
  → OBSERVED RESULT (outcome evidence, or explicitly unknown)
  → REFLECTION / CORRECTION (revise plan; preserve provenance)
```

Keep the philosophical origin separately labeled as **BELIEF / ATTRIBUTED CONCEPT**, not promoted to observation.
Keep **HYPOTHESIS** separate from confirmed eligibility and benefit.
Keep **ACTION / PROPOSED** separate from an action actually executed; execute only after independent authorization.
Keep **OBSERVED RESULT** separate from source observations about opportunities, user-reported intentions, and promised outcomes; a verified result requires an independent, appropriately attributed receipt.
Keep **CONTRADICTED** and **UNVERIFIED** visible where applicable. Do not silently delete conflicting evidence.

## Reproducible falsification protocol

**Hypothesis H1:** In a controlled set of user research tasks, a purpose → candidate → explicit evidence gate → human action → verified outcome explanation reduces mistaken eligibility/outcome claims compared with ordinary search summaries.

- **Baseline:** five prior comparable investigations without the lens, using the same rules for source freshness and sensitive data.
- **Treatment:** five comparable investigations with the E1 lens.
- **Measure:** false eligibility/outcome assertions (primary), time to useful source, unsupported causal links, human review burden, confirmed benefit receipts and corrections.
- **Falsify:** any path presenting a metaphysical claim as science; any funding/credential indicated as earned without independent issuer evidence; any action described as completed without a matching receipt; any sensitive portfolio source exposed; or a higher false-claim count than baseline.
- **Report honestly:** small sample cannot establish a general cognitive or physical law. Record negative and null results.
