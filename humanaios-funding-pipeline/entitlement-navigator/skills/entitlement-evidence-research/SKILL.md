# Entitlement Evidence Research

Use this skill to turn a general user situation into an evidence-bearing investigation of assets, benefits, assistance, financing, grants, procurement, estates, trust property, and ancestral claims.

## Method
1. Begin with a general statement of the user's objective. Do not assume genealogy, tribal status, business ownership, or benefit category.
2. Identify each legal applicant/owner node separately: individual, household, business, nonprofit, estate/heir, property, ancestral chain.
3. Ask the minimum next question that can eliminate or activate the most pathways.
4. Ingest user documents as evidence with provenance. Treat family trees and stories as research clues unless corroborated.
5. Research primary sources and resolve eligibility predicates one at a time.
6. Classify each pathway: RULE_MATCH, CONDITIONAL_MATCH, INVESTIGATE, INELIGIBLE, CLOSED_HISTORICAL, or GENERAL_OPPORTUNITY.
7. Produce an action plan ordered by: submission-ready rights/benefits; high-value evidence investigations; evidence gathering; general opportunities.
8. For genealogy/property branches, require: person identity → authoritative historical enrollment if relevant → parcel/allotment → title chain → probate/heirship → present ownership → associated trust funds. Never skip links.
9. Preserve contrary and negative evidence.
10. Request sensitive identifiers only when a named authoritative application/claim requires them.

## Output
Every result must state the applicant entity, current classification, supporting evidence, missing evidence, authoritative source, next action, and falsifier/change condition.
