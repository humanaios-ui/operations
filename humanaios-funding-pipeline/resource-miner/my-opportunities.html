    Evidence and limitations

    Observed Gmail welcome messages and calendar receipts are separate from human reports. This offline projector checks declared source types but does not cryptographically verify providers, read your accounts, submit applications, or grant permission to act.

    Only the sanitized journey is sent to the local projection API; raw linked evidence is not transmitted to the server. Browser storage is optional, local to this browser profile and not encrypted. Private mail links and quoted messages may be sensitive; avoid passwords, tokens and unnecessary personal details. Export files contain these references in plaintext and should stay private.
