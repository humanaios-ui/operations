Night's instruction names "the Section F.1 ruling" but does not state which reading it takes, and Z1 did
not previously propose specific replacement text. The substance of a Z2 ruling is not Z1's to infer, so

**The tension:** F.1 reads "A canonical-source fetch fails or returns unexpected data." Amended §A.1
says halt only if **both** the WGS primary and the haioscc secondary fail. Read literally and together,
a session whose haioscc fetch fails must proceed under §A.1 and stop under F.1.

- **(a) F.1 is general, §A.1 is specific.** The specific governs where it applies; F.1 continues to
  cover every other canonical source (`CURRENT.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `REGISTERED.md`). Smallest change:
  add a clause to F.1 excepting sources with their own halt rule. Keeps F.1's reach.
- **(b) F.1 inherits the both-fail rule** for any source that has a declared primary/secondary pair,
  stated once in F.1 rather than per-source. Fewer places to drift, but rewrites a halt condition that
  currently applies cleanly to single-source fetches.

Z1's read is **(a)** — it is the smaller change and F.1's breadth is doing real work for the other three
canonical fetches — but this is stated as a recommendation, not a decision.
