## Claim

- **claim:** Of 1,752 git-tracked files in this repo, 777 (44%) are Markdown versus ~520 actual code files (.py/.js/.ts/.jsx/.tsx) — Markdown alone outnumbers all code combined. `z1-inbox/` holds 147+ files, a large share of which are ratification/ruling ceremony (e.g. 30+ `Q-BOARD-RULING-*` files from a single week). `REGISTERED.md` itself is substantially self-referential — entries correcting misfilings of earlier entries (e.g. IC-060/IC-061 misfiled as IC-035/IC-037, later corrected). Not one step ever executed, and this went unnoticed until fixed.
- **class:** Structural/process — not a single incident but a pattern: significant ongoing investment in governance ceremony and self-correction relative to the volume of shipped, user-facing product. Of the 31-repo ecosystem, only one thing (ACAT) is both live and meant to face external users today; everything else active is internal tooling, auditing, or research (see Q-ACAT-DISTRIBUTION-PLAYS-01, filed same session, for the full inventory).

- **why this matters here:** the source article's central warning is spending months on infrastructure/process around a thing instead of getting the thing in front of real users. This repo's governance layer is exactly that kind of infrastructure investment, and it is currently much larger than the product layer it exists to govern.
