## 2. Hypotheses (each with falsifier; none registered until hashed)

**H-CAND-DIVINER-01** — The diviner/cunning-person recurs across lineage-independent traditions as the *feedback read* component: a licensed, marginal specialist who reads signs on the group's behalf and can be turned on when readings fail.
Falsifier: fewer than 8 independent traditions, or the "turned-on-when-wrong" feature below 0.60 co-occurrence under a frozen coding sheet.
Tier: CLAIM+LINK (Evans-Pritchard 1937; Peek 1991; Moore 1957; Marwick 1964).

**H-CAND-SPELL-01** — The spell is the *staged procedure* component: exact sequence, preconditions, a stated effect (a prediction), and built-in failure attribution.
Falsifier: a documented spell tradition lacking either the exact-repetition rule or a failure-attribution rule.
Tier: CLAIM+LINK (Malinowski 1935; Tambiah 1968; Frazer; Mooney 1891).
Note: the spell is NOT a feedback read. It encodes a prediction; it does not read live state.

**H-CAND-CASCADE-01** — Feedback read + staged procedure + blame, *without* an external check, is self-amplifying, not self-regulating. The European hunts are the natural experiment (≈90,000 prosecuted, ≈45,000 executed; arrest came only from external legal checks — Parlement de Paris, Salazar Frías, torture restriction).
Falsifier: a large accusation cascade that stopped from inside the accusing system without an external procedural check.
Tier: CLAIM+LINK (Levack; Behringer; Midelfort; Doten-Snitker/Pfaff/Hsiao 2024; Soman; Henningsen).
This is the strongest new finding: **the external check is what keeps the other three from running away.** The jester is not decorative.

**H-CAND-FUSION-01** — The sacred clown (heyoka, koshare) is a documented single office fusing external check (licensed inversion) with feedback read (vision-licensed authority). Hyde's shaman/trickster contrast is the theoretical warrant.
Falsifier: ethnography showing the clown's license does not derive from ritual authority in ≥ 2 of the documented cases.
Tier: CLAIM (Radin 1956; Hyde 1998; Tedlock) — primary texts unread by Z1.

**Jester + witch as a European court pair: NOT CLAIMED.** Z1 synthesis only. Macbeth/Lear stay as illustration.
