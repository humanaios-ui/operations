## Body (LinkedIn character limit strategy: 3000 chars + image)

We stopped building a measurement tool. We built an **operating system** instead.

**The problem:** Multi-practice AI teams lack agreed authority. Measurement stays hidden. No one knows measurement uncertainty.

**The shift:** What if the problem wasn't *how to measure* AI systems, but *how to build systems that measure themselves?*

We built that. It's called **HumanAIOS**—infrastructure that governs while measuring. And we just open-sourced the measurement layer: **ACAT** (AI Behavioral Calibration Assessment Technology).

### Three Technical Zones

HumanAIOS uses three decision boundaries:

- **Zone 1** (Unilateral): Humans decide alone. AI executes fast. (Emergency response)
- **Zone 2** (Shared): Humans + AIs deliberate together. Both required. (Design review)
- **Zone 3** (Human-Final): Humans decide alone. AI is advisory. (Regulatory commitments)

The key: Zones are *technical gates*, not cultural norms. Breach attempts log as drift signals. The audit trail *proves* governance is real.

### Self-Measurement, Not External Audit

The system measures itself in real time:

- **Drift signals fire automatically** when authority boundaries are breached
- **Feedback loops close fast**: discovery → correction → verification in <4 hours
- **Audit trail is complete**: every Zone 2 decision logged, every Zone 3 call recorded

Measurement uncertainty is *published*, not hidden. EU AI Act demands this. We deliver it.
