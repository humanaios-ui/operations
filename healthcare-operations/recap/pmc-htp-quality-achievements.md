## 2. HTP operating environment — verified public program mechanics

Colorado HTP began in 2021 as a five-year HCPF-administered performance improvement program (scheduled through October 2026). The Colorado Healthcare Affordability and Sustainability Enterprise (CHASE) Board oversees the program. Hospitals' supplemental Medicaid payments are subject to documented reporting and performance incentives [R1–R3].

Public rules specify, among other components:
- Hospital application with interventions and measure selection.
- Implementation plan with milestones.
- Quarterly activity / measure / CHNE reporting.
- Milestone reports and permitted corrective plans.
- Sustainability planning.
- Performance and payment measures tied to program-year requirements.

**Interpretation guard:** “Improved HTP reporting” does **not** by itself demonstrate increased Medicaid payments, a realized grant, verified measure improvement, or personal credit. Obtain PMC-approved performance and payment receipts before asserting financial achievement.

**Terminology guard:** HTP (began 2021) must not be confused with Colorado's distinct **Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP)** awards announced in 2026 [R7]. A 2026 PMC RHTP award is **not** retrospective evidence of 2021–2024 HTP funding attributable to this role.
