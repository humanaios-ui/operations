# HumanAIOS Collaboration Management System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Established:** 2026-07-22  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-22

---

## Overview

Complete collaboration management infrastructure for HumanAIOS LLC. Five integrated systems ensure clear roles, agreements, project tracking, decision transparency, and relationship mapping.

**Key Goal:** Scale collaboration from two-person (Carly + Demarius) to multi-party research ecosystem while maintaining trust, clarity, and accountability.

---

## Five Core Systems

### 1. **Collaborator Profiles** — `collaborators/`
**Who:** Directory of all active collaborators and partner organizations

**Contents:**
- **README.md** — Master index (people, orgs, contact info, status)
- **CARLY_R_ANDERSON.md** — Founder & PI profile
- **DAVID_EMPIRICA.md** — Empirica mesh support
- **DEMARIUS_J_LAWSON.md** — Research partner & evaluator
- **LONGVIEW_FOUNDATION.md** — Funder profile

**When to use:**
- Finding contact info for a collaborator
- Understanding someone's role in HumanAIOS
- Confirming decision authority for a given area
- Adding a new collaborator

---

### 2. **Collaboration Agreements** — `collaborator-ops/agreements/`
**What:** MOUs, partnerships, IP terms, contribution agreements

**Contents:**
- **README.md** — Registry of all active agreements + templates
- **LONGVIEW_GRANT_TERMS.md** — Grant conditions, budget, reporting (pending award)
- **MODE_AI_PARTNERSHIP.md** — Research collaboration + evaluation partnership
- **DEMARIUS_LABS_EVALUATION.md** — ACAT evaluation + governance consultation

**When to use:**
- Clarifying what a partner is committed to
- Resolving disputes about terms
- Onboarding new collaborators (use templates)
- Preparing funder reports (check grant terms)

**Key decision points:** IP ownership, attribution, termination, compensation

---

### 3. **Active Projects** — `collaborator-ops/active-projects/`
**Who's doing what:** Project tracking with collaborator assignments, deliverables, timelines

**Contents:**
- **README.md** — Project registry + template
- **LONGVIEW_GRANTS_RFP.md** — Main 18-month program (3 initiatives)
- **ACAT_EXPANSION.md** — Benchmark development (Months 1-6)
- [Additional projects as they launch]

**When to use:**
- Understanding who's working on what
- Tracking project milestones
- Coordinating between collaborators
- Assigning work to new team members

**Key elements per project:** Lead, collaborators, deliverables, timeline, risks, success criteria, communication sync

---

### 4. **Decision Log** — `collaborator-ops/decisions/`
**Transparency:** Record of collaboration decisions, approvals, disputes

**Contents:**
- **README.md** — Decision registry + template + escalation process

**When to use:**
- Documenting a decision that affects multiple parties
- Resolving disagreements (logged + escalation path)
- Tracking who approved what (audit trail)
- Learning from past decisions

**Logged decisions:** Budget levels, partnership formalization, co-I assignments, major scope changes

---

### 5. **Ecosystem Map** — `collaborator-ops/ecosystem-map/`
**Why it matters:** Visual + structural view of HumanAIOS collaboration network

**Contents:**
- **README.md** — Network diagram, trust paths, communication channels, conflict screening

**When to use:**
- Onboarding new collaborators (how does HumanAIOS fit in the ecosystem?)
- Planning communication strategy
- Identifying credibility paths (for funders, partners)
- Spotting conflicts of interest before they become problems

## Maintenance Schedule

**Weekly:**
- Update project milestones in active-projects/ (if changed)
- Note any collaboration blockers

**Monthly:**
- Sync with collaborators; update their profile status if changed
- Review active decisions for closure/escalation

**Quarterly (or as-needed):**
- Archive completed projects
- Formalize new partnerships (move from "pending" to "active" agreements)
- Update ecosystem map if network changes

**After Longview decision:**
- Activate full project plan in LONGVIEW_GRANTS_RFP.md
- Formalize Mode AI + Demarius Labs agreements
- Create sub-project files for each of the 3 initiatives

## Key Principles

1. **Transparency:** All decisions affecting multiple parties are logged + visible
2. **Clarity:** Agreements spell out IP, attribution, terms (no ambiguity)
3. **Accountability:** Who decides what is always clear (decision authority)
4. **Scalability:** System works with 2 collaborators or 20 (just add files)
5. **Trust:** Collaborators see mutual commitments in writing; no surprises
