## Part I — My read of REGISTERED.md + the direction (you asked for this)

**REGISTERED.md is your single most under-used attraction asset.** It's an append-only, self-auditing research ledger — F-class findings, and crucially **IC-class integrity corrections** where you publish your *own* process violations and methodological errors. "Findings are not deleted; they are superseded with a forward pointer." Almost nobody in AI does this in public. **The direction, as I read it, is two-layered:**

**The strategic tension to write *through*, not around:** a reader arriving for the AI-safety research doesn't yet see how it connects to recovery-funded worker co-ops. The series is where that bridge gets built — carefully, without turning research into a pitch.

**The public frame is already set, and it's good:** the site leads with *"The Witness Problem — ask an AI how honest it is; it answers confidently; it has no way to know."* Lead public content with **"The Witness Problem,"** and keep **"self-description calibration"** as the academic/SEO synonym underneath. 1. **Notes, 3–5×/week (20–30 min/day).** Short, specific observations from REGISTERED.md — a single finding, a chart, a "here's a correction we filed." Notes drive the majority of sub growth under 10K. This is the daily habit that compounds.
2. **Recommendations — seek and give.** Get recommended by adjacent AI-safety newsletters (CAIS *AI Safety Newsletter*, Joe Carlsmith, and the [EA Forum newsletter list](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/hsmh4fD8Dbkzvdehk/list-of-ai-safety-newsletters-and-other-resources)). One rec from a 5K+ pub = 50–200 subs. This is Tradition-11-clean (you're recommended *into* the niche, not self-promoting).
3. **Cross-post every piece to X (@HumanAIOS) + LinkedIn (/in/humanaios)** — the same surfaces the identity work is wiring up. Repurpose the anchor argument as a thread.
4. **Canonical linking on every post:** hub (`humanaios.ai`), preprint **DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21135723`**, dataset (`HumanAIOS/acat-assessments`). This is how the content engine feeds the entity graph we built in P0-3.

**Cadence recommendation:** **biweekly** long posts (6 parts ≈ 12 weeks) + Notes 3–5×/week. Weekly is the statistical sweet spot, but for a solo operator *consistency you can sustain* beats a fast start you can't. Lock the day (e.g., every other Tuesday).
