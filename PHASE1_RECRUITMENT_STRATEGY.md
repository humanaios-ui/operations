## Recruitment Message (Universal)

### For Employers
```
Subject: Help us test fair research task coordination

Hi [Name],

We're launching a transparent, fair research task marketplace where you can:
- Post research tasks (writing, data analysis, design, validation, etc.)
- Tap into a curated pool of vetted researchers/writers/contributors
- Pay directly to workers (no middleman extraction)
- See full breakdown of fees (transparency is our principle)

Why try it:
- We use behavioral assessment (ACAT) to match task-person fit, reducing bad matches
- All earnings are transparent (you'll see exactly what workers earned)
- No hidden fees or predatory practices (we publish monthly reports)
- Built on proven platforms: GitHub + Open Collective + Substack

Cost: 5% coordination fee on your task budget (employer pays, not worker)
Example: $500 task → $465 goes to worker, $25 to platform ops

Want to post a pilot task? We're looking for 3-5 employers to test the system 
with sample tasks ($200-750 range). Full transparency on results + feedback.

### For Workers
```
Subject: Earn money doing research work (transparent, fair platform)

Hi [Name],

We're launching a research task marketplace designed to be fair:
- Employers post tasks directly (writing, analysis, design, coding, validation)
- You apply + get selected based on skills/fit
- You get paid directly to your account (no extraction, no payment blocks)
- We publish everything: what you earned, what we kept, where the money went

Why it's different:
- No competition-based model (only winner gets paid) — every task has clear success criteria
- Fast payment: Open Collective escrow (1-5 business days)
- Behavioral assessment matching: we try to send you tasks where you'll succeed

Earnings: Varies by task ($200-750 typical), depends on complexity + deadline
Frequency: Ongoing (post tasks weekly, you apply to what fits)

How to apply:

3. Comment with your profile/portfolio
4. We'll select based on fit + relevance

First tasks go live [DATE]. Early applicants get priority.
