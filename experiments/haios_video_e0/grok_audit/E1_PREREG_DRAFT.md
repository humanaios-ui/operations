## Randomized study (consenting adults only)
- Arms: A article, B conventional video script, C Witness video script (same frozen propositions; treatment = evidence navigation).
- Primary: correct answers on a frozen question set. Secondary: evidence selection, limitations retention, false-ready rate, confidence calibration, perceived pressure/autonomy, time, accessibility, dropout, opt-out.
- Analysis plan, exclusions and missing-data handling are fixed before launch. No causal claim from platform traffic, views or engagement.
- Nonparticipation, dissent and exit are preserved; withdrawal deletes the participant's data per the approved retention plan.

## Observational public retrieval study (separate; only after an approved release)
- 12 frozen query intents (4 factual, 4 limitations, 4 authority/corrections); record engine, configuration, version, timestamp, retrieved source, exact citation, unsupported claims, caveat omissions.
- Repeat at days 7, 14, 21, 30. Distinguish indexability from citation, nonresponse from wrong answer, visibility from correctness.
- One correction challenge on a harmless synthetic fixture; keep the prior version; measure time to accurate citation.
