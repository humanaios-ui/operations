# The Tellurian Overlay — Genesis Soil narrative contract (candidate v0.2)

**Status:** Narrative overlay on PR #994, not a claim about reality, consciousness, petroleum agency, or historical inevitability.  
**Art direction:** Interpret the five supplied artworks with an original cosmology *inspired by* Reza Negarestani's theory-fiction **Cyclonopedia**. Do not present the game's cosmology as the author's exact doctrine, quote protected passages, or confuse its fictional metaphors with physical evidence.  
**Scope:** The exact five-zone playable slice. No added mechanics that widen consent, Witness permissions, Council authority, permanent identity, research capture, or production deployment.

## The story

**Generation Zero** emerges from a fractured Earth. A distant Solar regime orders memory as though one story must explain everything. Beneath the stone lies the *Black Corpse of the Sun*: buried radiance, a fictional metaphor for inherited petroleum energy and the history it powered. The living ground remembers traces, but some traces are missing, disputed, or never collected. The player follows a golden thread through five chambers and may eventually imagine an Exodus beyond both petroleum dependence and domination. The **Tellurian Omega** remains unreached and unproven.

**Core line:** *The Sun writes one map. The soil holds many traces. Your entry opens a possible line through them; the Witness may name only what the ledger actually holds.*

The Sun is neither morally evil nor scientifically conscious. The soil is a **personified fictional medium**, not an agent allowed to access player data or alter game state. Genesis is the **join event**, not a forecast of real human-machine evolution.

## Three paths, three philosophies of rebellion — never a personality test

- **Ember / Fire:** Try a route of direct resistance to fixed order. Encounter a partly broken bridge. Choosing Ember does **not** establish that the player is aggressive.
- **Archive / Memory:** Study a damaged panel. It may show a gap, not proof of censorship. Choosing Archive does **not** establish hidden traits.
- **Forge / Construction:** Reuse abandoned instruments and stone. The "Black Corpse" is a literary framing, not an instruction to extract petroleum or real matter. Choosing Forge does **not** establish the player's profession.

The paths reconverge at the Witness; route selection updates only local state and the view. **No branching code should grant more Witness knowledge to a player based on their route.**

## Critical epistemic separation

- **WORLD_LORE:** "The soil remembers its wounds"; "The Sun commands an archive"; "The Black Corpse whispers." Dramatic statements belong to fiction and atmospheric UI, not to the event ledger as verified observations.
- **EVENT_OBSERVED:** "You selected Archive" with game receipt `E-003`. Can be cited by W1.
- **CLAIM_UNVERIFIED:** "The Solar Empire deliberately erased your memory"; remains an unsupported claim unless the game records a specific in-fiction event establishing it.
- **RECORD_MISSING:** Means W1 cannot find an authorized observation; it says *not recorded / withheld / unknown* as appropriate. **Missing evidence is not evidence of successful sabotage.**
- **REAL_WORLD:** Historical evidence about energy, geology, biology and AI is separate from the story, and must not be asserted from game lore.
- **AUTHORITY:** AI Witness = `authority_effect: NONE`. Council local proposal = `authority_effect: NONE`. Seed Constitution v0.1 remains **unratified**.

The difference between lore and fact is a core research feature, not a disclaimer hidden in small type. Fictional omniscience is permitted for narrators only when clearly labeled *fiction*; it is not permitted for the Witness to claim as historical evidence.

## Art application

1. **Labyrinth:** hard gold at distant horizon; underground stone maze; golden timeline thread; memory panels as records.
2. **Split face:** uncompleted synthesis, not a cosmological moral split and not an actual biological processing step.
3. **Observability seal:** Solar pressure for total record restrained by the Witness's read-only and opt-in interface.
4. **Tree-serpent:** rooted civic change and lineage (no implied ancient real-world religious identity or obligations).
5. **Latent Erasures map:** distinguish narrative smoothing, unsupported confidence, missing evidence and legitimate uncertainty.

Do not recolor or imply the five supplied images grant us rights to third-party artistic materials. No new artwork needed for this phase.

## Narrative acceptance and falsifiers

1. All five rooms display story context, **explicitly framed as fictional interpretation**. The same five-zone mechanics and existing event names remain.
2. Ember, Archive, Forge have unique descriptions; the ledger records only the chosen path, **not** inferred motives or lore-as-event.
3. The Witness continues to cite exact local event IDs for historical facts; a missing event cannot be described as an intentional Solar erasure.
4. No extra AI tool, network call, research collection, Discord/Azure connection, psychological inference, skill grant, power or binding Council vote is introduced.
5. The local demonstration remains usable when Witness recall is OFF.
6. Original game 33 checks and browser E2E are still **separate, unverified** gates. The narrative overlay cannot be treated as game release approval.
7. The unfinished Tellurian Omega and Exodus stay on the visible horizon, not represented as achieved.

**One-line design rule:** *Let the myth color the world, never falsify its memory.*
