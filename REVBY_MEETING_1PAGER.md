# HumanAIOS: Operating System for Self-Measuring Teams
## One-Page Brief for Revby Meeting

---

## What This Is

**HumanAIOS is infrastructure for building observable operating systems** — systems that measure themselves, detect when they drift from stated principles, and self-correct through automated feedback loops.

Not a tool. Not a product. An **operating system foundation** being developed at TRL 2-3, currently operational with a live platform (FLTA), measurement framework (ACAT), and governance model (Zone 1/2/3 authority + audit trail).

---

## Core Innovation

**Three-layer architecture that coordinates humans and AI agents while maintaining integrity:**

1. **Governance layer** — Who can execute what (Zone 1: autonomous, Zone 2: co-decide, Zone 3: founder)
2. **State management** — Version-controlled decisions accessible to all participants
3. **Self-measurement** — Real-time drift detection + automated correction loops

**Why it matters for capital:** Most systems are measured from *outside*. This one measures itself. That's rare, and it's what enables safe autonomous execution at scale.
