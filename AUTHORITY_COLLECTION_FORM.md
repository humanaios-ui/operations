## What This Is

Each of the 10 foundation practices must designate a **Z2 Authority** — the decision-maker who will:
- Review governance PRs within SLA (24h-72h depending on type)
- Answer 5 gate questions before approving changes
- Represent your practice in cross-practice governance decisions
