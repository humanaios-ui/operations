## How We Differ (Fair Orchestration vs. Extraction)

| Factor | Exploitative Model | Empirica Orchestration |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Worker earnings guarantee** | Hidden/fabricated | Direct from employer, visible |
| **Payment security** | Blocked, delayed | Open Collective escrow, fast |
| **Coordination fee** | Hidden (90%+ extraction) | Transparent (5%) + 2% back to workers |
| **Reporting** | Secret financials | Public monthly reports |
| **Employer trust** | Black box matching | Behavioral assessment + transparent criteria |

**Principle:** We win when workers win. Our fee (5%) is only valuable if we reliably match people to tasks where they succeed.

## Reader Q&A

**Q: How do I apply for a task?**
A: [Link to GitHub org]. Find a task that matches your skills, comment with your portfolio, wait for selection.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Upon task completion, we release funds via Open Collective (1-5 business days depending on your payout method).

**Q: Do I owe taxes on earnings?**
A: [Link to tax FAQ]. You're an independent contractor; consult your accountant. If earnings > $600/year (US), you may receive a 1099.

**Q: Why do you take 5%?**
A: [Link to finance breakdown]. 5% covers: platform operations, worker verification bonuses (2% returned), payment processing, and research infrastructure.

**Q: What's the verification bonus?**
A: If your work is strong, we send a bonus (typically $5-50) as recognition. It's not guaranteed, but quality workers who repeat tend to earn them.

**Q: Can I see the matching algorithm?**
A: We're working with humanaios to publish our behavioral assessment methodology. Coming soon (pending mesh review).
