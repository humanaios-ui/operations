## Project Structure Template

Each active project should have:
- **ACTIVE_PROJECTS/{project-name}.md** with full details
- Collaborator assignments (who, what role, time commitment)
- Deliverables & milestones
- Success criteria & metrics
- Risks & mitigation
- Links to related agreements

---

## Process

1. **New project:** Create Active-Projects/{name}.md before starting
2. **Assignment:** Assign lead + collaborators; notify them via collaborators registry
3. **Updates:** Monthly milestone tracking in the project file
4. **Completion:** Log completion in Decision Log; archive to completed-projects/
5. **Lessons:** Capture lessons learned for next collaboration

---

## Key Questions per Project

- **Lead clarity:** Who has final decision authority?
- **Collaborator capacity:** Do they have time? What's their other commitment level?
- **Deliverables:** What's "done"? Who approves?
- **Communication:** How often do leads sync? With collaborators?
- **Escalation:** If project is at risk, who decides next steps?
