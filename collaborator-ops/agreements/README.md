## Agreement Templates

- **Research Collaboration MOU:** Standard terms for joint research projects
- **IP Assignment:** Who owns code, data, publications
- **Contribution Agreement:** Team member contributions and compensation
- **Funder Reporting:** Grant-specific reporting & compliance

---

## Process

1. **Proposal:** Draft agreement in pull request (collaborator-ops/agreements/)
2. **Review:** Both parties review; clarify any gaps
3. **Ratification:** Sign-off logged in Decision Log with date + version
4. **Active:** Linked from Collaborator Profile
5. **Amendment:** New version if terms change; old version archived

---

## Key Decision Points (All Agreements)

Before finalizing any agreement, clarify:
- **IP ownership** — who can publish/open-source findings?
- **Attribution** — how are collaborators credited?
- **Termination** — what ends the agreement? Notice period?
- **Escalation** — who decides if conflicts arise?
- **Duration** — term length, renewal, renegotiation cadence?
