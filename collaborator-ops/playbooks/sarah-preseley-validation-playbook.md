## Why This Playbook

Before we invite real collaborators into HumanAIOS (researchers, domain experts, technical partners), we need to test the playbook that guides that process. Sarah Preseley is an internal collaborator who's already contributed to HumanAIOS work—she's a perfect test candidate because she understands the context and can give honest feedback without us worrying about scaring off a real prospect.

This playbook walks through **Stages 1–2 of the full onboarding framework:**
- **Stage 1:** Identification & Vetting (Did we pick the right person? Are they mesh-ready?)
- **Stage 2:** Outreach & Proposal (Do our invitation materials work? Are expectations clear?)

We skip Stages 3–5 (Clarification, Active, Transition) because this is a validation, not a real engagement.

## Stage 2: OUTREACH & PROPOSAL (Praxic)

### Step 2.1 — Prepare Collaboration Packet

**What Goes in the Packet:**

#### Brief on HumanAIOS Mission & Current Work
- [ ] **What we do:** 1-paragraph mission statement
  - *Current: "HumanAIOS develops tools and frameworks to assess AI system behavior, calibration, and potential moral status."*
- [ ] **Current focus areas:** 3–5 bulleted active projects
  - *Current (2026-07 snapshot): ACAT expansion, Metaculus forecasting ops, Digital Minds governance research*
- [ ] **How collaborators fit:** Why we need Advisors / Co-Investigators / Core team right now

*Validation question: Is this level of detail right? Does it make you (Sarah) understand what HumanAIOS is doing?*

#### Role Description + Time Commitment
- [ ] **Advisor role specifics:**
  - Read-only access to findings & decision logs
  - Monthly async check-in (15–30 min)
  - Consultation on decisions (you advise, we decide)
  - Est. 5–10 hrs/mo
- [ ] **What's expected:** Review findings, ask clarifying questions, flag concerns
- [ ] **What's NOT expected:** You don't have to agree with every decision; you're a second opinion, not a veto

*Validation question: Is "Advisor" clear? Would a real external candidate understand the role?*

#### Mesh Discipline Expectations
- [ ] Pull when uncertain (ask instead of guess)
- [ ] Push when convergent (share grounded findings)
- [ ] Ack completions (reply even if "can't help")
- [ ] Don't drop threads (keep conversations open)
- [ ] Log findings if you discover something (use empirica finding-log)

*Validation question: Is mesh discipline too much to ask of an Advisor? Should we simplify for external collaborators?*

#### Success Metrics & Deliverables
- [ ] What does success look like for this collaboration?
  - *Example: "You provide 2–3 rounds of feedback per quarter; we deliver a clear evolution of our work based on your input."*
- [ ] How often do we check in? (monthly? quarterly?)
- [ ] How long is this engagement? (3 months? 6 months? ongoing?)
- [ ] What's the off-ramp if either side wants to stop?

*Validation question: Are these metrics measurable? Would you (Sarah) know if we succeeded?*

## Walkthrough Call Script (Carly + David + Sarah)

**Duration:** ~60 min  
**Participants:** Carly (leading), David (governance input), Sarah (feedback)  
**Format:** Sync call; recording optional (ask Sarah)

### Opening (5 min)
- Welcome Sarah, thank her for participating
- Explain: "We're walking through what the real onboarding process would feel like. You're our guinea pig. Honest feedback only—tell us what's confusing."

### Stage 1 Deep Dive (20 min)
- Walk through **Identification & Vetting** as if Sarah is a real candidate
- Ask Sarah: 
  - *"Does this vetting process feel invasive? Reasonable? Missing anything?"*
  - *"Would you want to say 'no' at this point, or does it feel like we're picking the right fit?"*
- David adds governance input: 
  - *"Gate #1 should sit here; approval looks like [X]; timeline is [Y]"*

### Stage 2 Deep Dive (25 min)
- Walk through **Outreach & Proposal** as if we're inviting Sarah
- Show her the collaboration packet, role description, mesh discipline expectations
- Ask Sarah:
  - *"Is the role clear? What would you ask for clarification?"*
  - *"Does mesh discipline feel reasonable for an Advisor? Overwhelming? Not enough?"*
  - *"Would you say yes to this invitation? Why or why not?"*
- David adds governance input:
  - *"Gate #2 sits here; any special routing for external candidates?"*

### Success Metrics & Feedback (10 min)
- Debrief:
  - *"What's the 1–2 highest-priority thing we need to fix before real outreach?"*
  - *"What surprised you about this process?"*
  - *"If we run this with [real candidate] next month, what should we remember from today?"*

### Close (5 min)
- Thank Sarah, confirm feedback submission timeline (2026-07-29–30)
- Next steps: "We'll iterate based on your feedback and finalize by 2026-07-31"
